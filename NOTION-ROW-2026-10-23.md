## Bio

beehiiv's CEO posted the full backlog of what it takes to clone beehiiv. The next day, someone shipped one.

Marc Lou had paid beehiiv for two years. First yearly bill $1,000, renewal headed for $4,000 after the third price raise. He churned on Oct 9, open-sourced his replacement on Oct 10, and launched MailCheap.ai on Oct 15 at $19/month — $109 is what beehiiv Lite wanted for the same 4,000 subscribers.

Honest admission: I don't think most of us should do this. Deliverability, bounce handling and billing edge cases are where the weekends actually go, and his own delivery number comes with a "not enough data yet" attached. But a vendor that raises prices three times in two years is now shipping its own competitors.

Which tool would you rebuild first?
